// 01 about

I'm a student researcher motivated by project-based learning and hands-on experimentation. I enjoy building scalable, reliable solutions that bridge theoretical concepts with practical implementation — taking an idea from a notebook sketch to something that actually flies, serves, or renders.

My current work at the NIRO Lab focuses on integrating embedded systems, perception, and control for practical drone-based solutions — swarm coordination, lightweight payload delivery, and real-world deployment in agriculture and fisheries. Day to day I work with Pixhawk flight controllers, Raspberry Pi, and Arduino, along with MAVLink communication, ground control integration, and real-time monitoring.

I've completed CS50 (HarvardX), and I program in C, C++, Python, and Java. On the software side I build full-stack with React, Next.js, Node.js, and Django — mostly for control interfaces and visualization tools that support the robotics work.

I'm always open to research collaborations and interesting problems — if you're working on something at the edge of robotics and software, I'd love to hear about it.
